# "Everybody Parties… Except Balary" (60-second Roblox video script)

**Format:** Roblox lip-dub, deadpan narration with a sad shrug. Lonely shots of Balary cut against shots of everyone else partying, with "huss" breathing over the basketball dribbling.
**Character:** Balary. Blocky Roblox avatar, oversized hoodie, flat face, always holding a basketball.
**Tone:** Deadpan, a little sad, funny because he plays it so straight.

## Voiceover-only read (for recording)

My name is Balary.
In this town… everybody loves partying. Every weekend, they all go to Zanzibar… with the Resource Guy.
And on Monday? Everybody pulls up to MakCity… with Berri Boy.
Everybody goes out partying… … except me.
Huss. Huss. Huss.
They're dancing on the beach… I'm doing layups. Huss. Huss.
They got Berri Boy on the aux… I got this ball. Huss, huss, huss.
One day… I'll go to Zanzibar too. … But not today. Huss.

## Editing notes
- Line each "huss" up with a ball bounce. That sync is where the joke lands.
- Hold 0.5s of silence after "except me" before the scratch.
- Put a low-pass filter on the party audio whenever the camera is on Balary, so it sounds far away.

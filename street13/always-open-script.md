## Video prompts

Every clip: vertical 9:16, cinematic commercial food footage, real-world
photographic look, warm tungsten and neon light against deep night shadows,
brand palette of signal red, mustard yellow and kraft brown. No voice, no
music in the clips (voiceover and amapiano are added in the edit).

### Shot 1 — Hook (0–3s) · two clips

**1A (2s)** — Vertical 9:16 cinematic night shot of a quiet, empty Abuja
street at 3 a.m. A wide road lined with low commercial buildings, every shop
shuttered with roller doors pulled down, a few sodium streetlights casting
orange pools on the tarmac, palm trees and dusty kerbs, no people, no cars.
Faint haze in the air. Camera locked off on a tripod with a very slow push
forward. Ambient sound only: crickets, a distant generator hum. No text, no
logos, no people.

**1B (1s)** — Vertical 9:16 close-up of a hand holding a smartphone in a dark
bedroom, the screen glow lighting the fingers, lock screen showing a large
clock. Slight handheld drift. *Tip: put the 3:00 AM time on as an overlay in
the edit; generators often get clock digits wrong.* Add the stomach-growl SFX
here.

### Shot 2 — Reveal (3–7s) · two clips

**2A (1.5s)** — *Image-to-video from `refs/storefront.jpg`.* Vertical 9:16 night
shot of this exact storefront: red and yellow facade, giant traffic-light
column on the left, illuminated red sign panel, brick-tiled service window
with warm light inside. The sign and traffic lights flicker and buzz on from
darkness, then hold steady and bright against the night sky. Camera locked.
Keep the facade, colours and layout exactly as in the reference. No added
text.

**2B (2.5s)** — *Image-to-video from `refs/loaded-fries.jpg`.* Vertical 9:16
slow-motion macro of loaded fries in a yellow paper tray printed with red
graphics, on a dark textured surface, lit by a hard warm side light. Thick
creamy pink sauce and glossy red barbecue sauce pour in ribbons over the
fries and crispy chicken pieces, then toppings land and bounce. Shallow
depth of field, steam rising. Camera very slow push in. Sizzle sound only.

### Shot 3 — Food montage (7–11s) · three clips cut on the beat

**3A (1.3s)** — *Image-to-video from `refs/burger.jpg`.* Vertical 9:16 studio

**3B (1.3s)** — Vertical 9:16 extreme close-up of a chicken cheese burger being
bitten, crunchy golden fried chicken fillet and melted cheese pulling, crumbs

**3C (1.4s)** — Vertical 9:16 slow-motion shot of crispy chicken wings tossed
in a steel bowl, glossy red-orange sauce coating them and flicking off in
droplets, warm kitchen light, dark background. Sauce-splash sound.

### Shot 4 — End card (11–15s) · one clip + graphic

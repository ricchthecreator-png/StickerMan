# Angle

**Working title:** Every Way GTA 6's Cops Are Smarter Than GTA 5's

**Spine:** In GTA 5, the cops chase *where you are*. In GTA 6, they chase *who you are*.

**Systems (chapters), each causing the next:**
1. The Report: nobody comes until someone tells them → *so who's telling them?*
2. The Witnesses: people and cameras → *and what do they tell the cops? What you look like.*
3. The Description: the shirt, the partner, the gun → *and what you're driving.*
4. The Car: trackers, cameras, trunks → *the car is also how you run.*
5. The Pursuit: six stars, the spike strip, the dog → *and when you finally break line of sight...*
6. The Search: star states, heat that lingers → *some of it doesn't fade at all.*
7. The Profile: the record that never resets.

Then: rumour pile → flip side (withheld loop paid off) → ending.

**Cold-open contrasts:** forget you / remember your shirt; hat works / hat does nothing; respray / they know the car; cops on your minimap / nowhere on it; five stars / six; you get away / a dog gets you.

**Open loops:**
1. Structural: "every one of these feeds the next one" → paid off when the ending walks the chain back.
2. Withheld: "there's one thing GTA 5 gave you in every chase that GTA 6 took away completely" → the minimap police blips, paid off in Flip Side with the music drop.

**Callbacks:** the shirt (cold open → Ch3 → ending → final line); the trunk (Ch3 → Ch4); the dog (cold open → Ch5 → ending); "Professional" (Ch1 joke → Ch7); the empty field (Ch1 → Ch6).
